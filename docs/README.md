# OctoAcme Project Management Process Documentation

## Overview

The OctoAcme project management framework guides how we plan, execute, and deliver projects with a focus on customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions.

OctoAcme applies a structured lifecycle approach to all cross-functional projects that deliver product features, services, or integrations. Our processes emphasize:
- **Customer-first delivery**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative execution**: Deliver small, testable increments rather than large monolithic releases
- **Clear ownership**: Every project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead responsible for success
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence and user feedback
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

## Project Lifecycle Stages & Documentation

Navigate the OctoAcme project management process by stage:

### 1. Initiation
**When**: Starting a new project idea or feature proposal  
**Purpose**: Validate business need, align stakeholders, and decide go/no-go for planning  
**Read**: [Project Initiation Guide](./octoacme-project-initiation.md)

### 2. Planning
**When**: Converting an approved initiative into an actionable plan  
**Purpose**: Break work into shippable increments, identify dependencies, and align timelines  
**Read**: [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md)

### 3. Execution & Tracking
**When**: Managing day-to-day execution and progress tracking  
**Purpose**: Coordinate team rhythm, manage quality, and escalate blockers  
**Read**: [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)

### 4. Risk Management & Communication
**When**: Managing risks, dependencies, and stakeholder updates  
**Purpose**: Identify and mitigate risks, communicate status, and escalate issues  
**Read**: [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md)

### 5. Release & Deployment
**When**: Preparing and deploying features to production  
**Purpose**: Standardize releases, reduce risk, and improve observability  
**Read**: [Release & Deployment Guide](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md)

### 6. Retrospective & Continuous Improvement
**When**: Capturing learnings after sprints, releases, or milestones  
**Purpose**: Convert learnings into actionable improvements and track outcomes  
**Read**: [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)

## Key Artifacts Used Across OctoAcme Projects

- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Business case, goals, and success metrics
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — Timeline, milestones, and delivery sequencing
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized work items with acceptance criteria
- **Risk Register** — Identified risks, mitigation plans, and status
- **Status Reports** — Weekly updates on progress, blockers, and decisions needed
- **Retrospective Notes** — Learnings and action items for continuous improvement
